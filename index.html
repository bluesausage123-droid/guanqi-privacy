觀棋心徑 — 隱私權政策

最後更新:2026-09-15

一、我們收集什麼資料?

本應用程式不收集任何個人資料,也沒有自己的伺服器。

觀棋心徑(以下稱「本服務」)的洗牌、抽棋與解卦都在您的裝置上完成,您輸入的問題與卦象結果不會傳送到任何伺服器。

二、本服務不做的事

  • 不收集您的姓名、電話、Email、生日、IP 位址等個人識別資訊。
  • 不要求註冊帳號、不需登入。
  • 不使用 Google Analytics、Firebase Analytics、Facebook Pixel 等任何分析或追蹤工具。
  • 不嵌入廣告、不使用廣告 ID。
  • 不存取您的通訊錄、相簿、相機、麥克風或位置。

三、存在您裝置上的資料

為了讓功能正常運作,本服務會在您的裝置上保存下列資料。這些資料只存在您的裝置上,不會上傳。

  • 卦例:您的問題文字、問題類別、性別、抽到的棋子與解說全文;命盤另含婚姻狀態。單卦在解說顯示後自動保存;今日一卦與命盤在您按下「保存」時才保存。最多保存 200 則,超過時自動移除最舊的。
  • 今日一卦:當天抽到的卦與解說,讓您同一天重開看到同一卦。
  • 設定:每日提醒是否開啟、提醒時間。
  • 程式快取(Service Worker Cache):程式本身的檔案,用於沒有網路時也能使用。
  • sessionStorage:頁面載入失敗時用來避免無限重新整理,關閉 App 即清空。

解除安裝本 App,或在手機系統設定中清除本 App 的資料,以上資料會全部刪除。

四、通知與震動

  • 每日提醒:這是手機的本地通知,只有在您於「今日一卦」開啟後才會排程;Android 13 以上會先詢問您是否允許通知。排程與通知內容都在手機上處理,不經過任何伺服器。為了讓手機重新開機後仍能提醒,App 會使用「開機完成後恢復排程」的權限。您可以隨時在 App 內關閉,或在系統設定中關閉通知。
  • 震動:抽棋時的輕微震動只使用手機的震動功能,不收集任何資料。

五、第三方服務與連結

  • 開啟「預約深度諮詢」視窗時,App 會從 LINE 的伺服器(qr-official.line.me)載入一張 LINE 官方帳號的 QR Code 圖片;LINE 可能因此取得您的連線資訊(例如 IP 位址)。
  • 點選「LINE 加入好友預約」「追蹤 Instagram」「關於 Jacky」(portaly.cc)會開啟外部 App 或網站,之後適用該服務自己的隱私權政策。
  • Android 版的「隨喜支持」透過 Google Play 付款,付款資料由 Google 處理,本服務不會取得您的付款資訊。

除以上情況外,App 不會主動發起外部網路請求。

六、兒童隱私

本服務適合 13 歲以上使用者。本服務不向任何人(包括 13 歲以下兒童)收集個人資料。

七、政策變更

若本政策有任何更動,我們會在此頁面更新「最後更新」日期。

八、聯絡方式

對本政策有任何疑問,請透過 App 內「預約諮詢」的 LINE 官方帳號聯絡我們。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 2026-09-15

1. What data do we collect?

This app does not collect any personal data and has no server of its own. Shuffling, drawing and interpretation all run on your device. Your questions and results are never sent to any server.

2. What we do not do

  • No personally identifiable information (name, phone, email, birthday, IP) is collected.
  • No account, no login.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook Pixel, etc.).
  • No ads, no advertising ID.
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

3. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored only on your device and never uploaded:

  • Saved readings: your question text, category, gender, the drawn pieces and the full interpretation (life charts also include marital status). Single readings are saved automatically after the interpretation is shown; the daily reading and life charts are saved only when you tap "Save". Up to 200 readings are kept; the oldest are removed beyond that.
  • Today's reading: the reading of the day, so reopening the app on the same day shows the same reading.
  • Settings: whether the daily reminder is on and its time.
  • App cache (Service Worker Cache): the app's own files, for offline use.
  • sessionStorage: used to avoid endless reloads after a load failure; cleared when the app closes.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your system settings, deletes all of the above.

4. Notifications and vibration

  • Daily reminder: a local notification, scheduled only after you turn it on in "Today's Reading". Android 13+ asks for your permission first. Scheduling and content stay on your device and never pass through any server. To keep reminders after a reboot, the app uses the permission to restore schedules on boot. You can turn it off in the app or in system settings at any time.
  • Vibration: the light vibration when drawing pieces only uses the device's vibration feature and collects no data.

5. Third-party services and links

  • Opening the "Book a consultation" window loads a LINE QR Code image from LINE's server (qr-official.line.me); LINE may receive connection information such as your IP address.
  • Tapping the LINE, Instagram or "About Jacky" (portaly.cc) buttons opens an external app or website, which is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • On Android, "Support" payments are handled by Google Play; this app never receives your payment details.

Apart from the above, the app makes no external network requests.

6. Children's privacy

This app is suitable for users aged 13+. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes

Any updates to this policy will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

For any questions, please reach out via the LINE official account linked in the app's "Book a consultation" feature.
